This noteskin's meant for Pump, but here's a little secret: it works on Dance/Techno too! Just slip it in either folder and it'll be playable on that mode. Or if you're using Project OutFox, slip it in the global folder.

If you get a message saying the hold dimensions aren't even numbers just click OK and ignore the message; the noteskin will play properly.
